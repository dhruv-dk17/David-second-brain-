# 📖 How to Use "David" Every Single Day

Welcome to your user manual for **David**. Follow this guide to make David smarter, tighter, and more powerful every time you use it.

---

## 1. Opening David in Obsidian (Your Visual Command Center)
1. Open the **Obsidian** app on your laptop.
2. Select **"Open folder as vault"** and select `D:\second brain`.
3. Press `Ctrl + G` to open the **Graph View**:
   - You will see **INDEX** at the bright white center.
   - Yellow nodes are your **Projects** (Vinland, Deniel, Bombay Fastfood, Telecode).
   - Blue nodes are your **Knowledge Areas** (Career, Vibe Coding, SAP MM).
   - Red nodes are your **Goals** (Switzerland, MBA).
4. Click on any `.base` file in the left sidebar:
   - [[Projects.base]] — Live table of all your software and business projects.
   - [[Goals.base]] — Milestone tracker.
   - [[Thoughts.base]] — Your incubator of unwritten ideas.

## 2. Using David in AI Tools (Local & Web)

### In Google Antigravity / Cursor / Windsurf
- Keep `D:\second brain` open as your workspace.
- Antigravity reads [[GEMINI]], [[AGENTS]], and [[INDEX]] automatically.
- Just talk normally: *"David, what's my status on Vinland?"* or *"David, I have an idea for cold coffee packaging, add it."*

### In Claude Code (Terminal CLI)
- In PowerShell, run: `cd "D:\second brain"; claude`
- Claude Code immediately reads [[CLAUDE]] and executes commands with full context.

### In Claude.ai Web & ChatGPT (On Your Browser or Phone!)
1. Open terminal and run:
   ```powershell
   python "D:\second brain\generate_ai_bundle.py"
   ```
2. This refreshes **`DAVID_AI_CORE.md`** (a complete snapshot of your second brain).
3. Upload `DAVID_AI_CORE.md` to a **Claude Project** or **ChatGPT Custom GPT** called **"David"**.
4. Now you can chat with David from your phone while walking, working at Bombay Fastfood, or in class!

## 3. How to Keep David Updated (The 3 Simple Habits)

> **The Golden Rule**: David is a living brain. Never let an idea vanish in your head.

### Habit 1: Spontaneous Brain Dumps
When a new idea pops into your mind (like your Cold Coffee venture or a Vinland feature):
- Just tell your AI: *"Add this idea to David: [Idea description]"*
- The AI will automatically create a note in `Thoughts/Ideas/<Idea Name>.md` and link it to [[INDEX]]!

### Habit 2: Logging Work & Decisions
Whenever you write code, hit a milestone, or make a decision:
- Tell your AI: *"Update [[Vinland]]: finished the auth flow"*
- The AI updates the note in-place, bumps `last updated`, and keeps facts clean without duplication.

### Habit 3: Daily Capture
- Open `Journal/Daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md` (or press `Ctrl + O` -> type today's date).
- Jot down 3 quick bullet points of what you did or what you learned.

---

## 4. The Self-Compounding Loop
Every time you add a project, an exam result, or a client win, David's graph grows stronger. Future AI sessions will give you **10x more tailored advice** because David already knows your history, your constraints, and your ultimate dream of reaching Switzerland!
