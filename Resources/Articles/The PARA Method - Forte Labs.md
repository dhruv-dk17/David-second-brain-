# The PARA Method — Forte Labs

> [!info] Resource Overview
> Tiago Forte's universal system for organizing digital information based on actionability rather than rigid Dewey-decimal categories.

## Core Categorization
- **Projects**: Series of tasks linked to a goal, with a deadline.
- **Areas**: Sphere of activity with a standard to be maintained over time.
- **Resources**: Topic or theme of ongoing interest.
- **Archive**: Inactive items from the previous three categories.
