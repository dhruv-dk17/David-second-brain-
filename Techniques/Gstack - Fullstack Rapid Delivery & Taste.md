# Gstack — Fullstack Rapid Delivery & Design Taste

> [!info] Technique Summary
> Combining rapid fullstack execution with high-end visual design taste. Eliminates ugly, generic AI layouts in favor of tactile micro-animations, rich dark modes, and crisp typography hierarchy.

## Design Taste Guidelines
1. **Typography Hierarchy**: Use deliberate font pairings (Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, Outfit) with strict scale (Hero, H1, H2, Meta).
2. **Tactile Micro-Animations**: Use Framer Motion and GSAP for subtle stagger effects, layout transitions, and hover feedback (showcased in [[Dhruv Portfolio]]).
3. **Harmonious Color Palettes**: Sleek dark modes with curated accents (Nordic greens in [[Vinland]], amber/gold for highlights).
4. **Gapless Layouts & Bento Grids**: Clean visual sections that present high information density comfortably.
