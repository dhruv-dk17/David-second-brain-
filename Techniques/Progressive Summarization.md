# Progressive Summarization

> [!info] Method Summary
> A 5-layer distillation workflow designed by Tiago Forte that compresses notes without losing original context, making knowledge rapidly scannable by humans and AI agents.

## The 5 Layers
1. **Layer 1: Raw Capture** — Unedited notes, transcripts, or articles.
2. **Layer 2: Bold Passages** — Bold the most salient, high-impact statements.
3. **Layer 3: Highlights** — Highlight the best of the bolded passages.
4. **Layer 4: Executive Mini-Summary** — Write a 2-3 bullet point summary at the top in your own words.
5. **Layer 5: Remix / Output** — Turn the distilled insight into code, an architecture decision, or an essay.
