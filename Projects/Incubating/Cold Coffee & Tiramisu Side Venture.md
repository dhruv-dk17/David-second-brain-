# Cold Coffee & Tiramisu Side Venture

> [!info] Venture Concept
> A grassroots, high-margin food & beverage side business founded by **Dhruv and his close friends** to produce and sell handcrafted **Cold Coffee** and gourmet **Tiramisu Cakes** for steady supplementary income.

## Core Value Proposition
- **High-Demand Comfort Treats**: Cold brew/flavored cold coffee and authentic tiramisu cakes have high youth appeal and strong social media shareability.
- **Low Startup Overhead**: Lean batch production using home kitchens or micro-prep setups with friends.
- **Direct-to-Consumer (D2C)**: Initial sales through collegiate networks (SASCMA College), local events, pop-up stalls, and WhatsApp/Instagram orders.

## Financial & Operational Model
- **Target**: Generate supplemental weekly cash flow to reinvest into computing equipment, college funds, and SAP certifications.
- **Roles**:
  - Dhruv: Brand positioning, ordering automation, finances, and growth strategy.
  - Friends: Recipe crafting, packaging, direct fulfillment, and batch production.
