# Neuromorphic Computing & Advanced Architectures

> [!info] Research Domain
> In-depth scientific investigation into **Neuromorphic Computing** — hardware and algorithmic architectures designed to mimic the biological neuro-synaptic structures of the human brain.

## Core Concepts from Dhruv's Research
- **Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs)**: Information is encoded in the precise timing of discrete electrical pulses ("spikes") rather than continuous continuous-valued activations.
- **Ultra-Low Energy Efficiency**: Events only consume energy when spikes occur (event-driven computing), reducing power draw by orders of magnitude compared to traditional GPUs.
- **Non-von Neumann Architecture**: Memory (synapses) and processing (neurons) reside in the same physical location, breaking the traditional von Neumann memory bus bottleneck.
