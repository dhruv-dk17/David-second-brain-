# Area — Health & Peak Performance

> [!info] Purpose
> Maintaining physical stamina, cognitive sharpness, deep focus, and restorative sleep to sustain intense engineering and entrepreneurial output.

## Pillars
1. **Physical Training**: Consistent gym, strength conditioning, and movement.
2. **Cognitive Hygiene**: Limiting dopamine drain (combated via [[Reelstop]]), protecting morning deep-work blocks.
3. **Nutrition & Sleep**: Clean fueling and 7-8 hours of quality sleep.
